The mod jar bundles these libraries. Their licenses are in this folder.

| ICU4J | 79.1 | Unicode License V3 (`LICENSE-icu4j.txt`) | https://github.com/unicode-org/icu |
| Kuromoji (core and IPADIC dictionary) | 0.9.0 | Apache License 2.0 (`LICENSE-kuromoji.txt`, `NOTICE-kuromoji.md`) | https://github.com/atilika/kuromoji |

Speech models are not bundled. They are downloaded on first use from https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models
and are distributed under their own licenses (most are Apache License 2.0).
